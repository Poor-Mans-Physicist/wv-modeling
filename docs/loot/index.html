How the score is computed

Each chest rolls a base count b of 12–15 (equally likely), multiplied by 1 + 1.1·ln(1 + IQ) and capped at 54 rolls. Each roll picks one of the chest's pools by weight; pools above the common one scale with rarity.

  • Unmapped: four pools (base weights 70/80, 20, 8 or 4, 2). Every non-common pool scales by 1 + IR, so the omega share levels off near 2/30. There is no mythic pool, so the score is omega rolls only.
  • Mapped: the same pools plus a mythic pool (weight 0.00445). Omega scales by (1+IR)^(1 + 0.12·ramp) and mythic by (1+IR)^(1.70 + 0.151·ramp), with ramp = (log₁₀(1+IR) / log₁₀ 201)^1.4.
  • Score = omega rolls + 5 × mythic rolls per 1000 chests, averaged over wooden, gilded, living and ornate chests (the deck optimizer's Loot metric).

What this leaves out

  • IQ and IR are your total values as the chest sees them, in percent (+500% = 500).
  • Strongboxes, treasure / god chests and coin piles are not included.
  • No greed medallion, Aspect of Treasure, Luck potions or crystal / map loot modifiers beyond what you type in.
  • Massive Chests is modelled as intended (+20 cap, rarity halved), matching the deck optimizer.
  • Past about +2,290% IQ every chest hits the roll cap, so more IQ adds nothing.
Formulas from the Wold's Vaults addon (MythicLootScaling, MixinTieredLootTableGenerator) and the pack's chest loot tables. Part of wv-modeling.
